* **Test coupon (SPEC §10 step 7):** the loads on G0-G3 deliberately exceed the coupon rules (250 mA on +3V3, 500 mA on +5V) to exercise the slot switches; **G3 (3.33 Ohm on +5V) is for S1-S4 only**: on S5 the 2 A breaker would let it draw 1.5 A, 2.5 W per 1 W resistor (sheets/test_coupon.py; the silk next to the load says G3: S1-S4 ONLY).
* **JP1 (WP)** is an open solder jumper: leave it open to write the descriptor EEPROM, then bridge it with solder to write-protect it.
* **Copper for the loads:** +5V pre-routed on the outer layers (1.5 mm F.Cu to the loads, 2.0 mm B.Cu to the TP2 wire pad, for an external load up to the S5 breaker's 2.46 A maximum trip, briefly), G3's drain 1.0 mm F.Cu; +3V3 and the other load strings (335 mA at most) 0.20 mm with the In2 +3V3 pour; In1 carries no track (boards/test_coupon.py).
* **PCB:** 4 layers, 1.6 mm, FR-4, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner copper, JLC's standard 4-layer stack (JLC04161H-7628; no impedance control ordered), ENIG (HASL lead-free would do: the finest pitch is 0.65 mm), green mask, white silk. L2 (In1) is a solid GND plane (a no-track rule area keeps the routers off it), L3 (In2) a +3V3 pour.
* **Design rules used:** track 0.20 mm and clearance 0.15 mm by default (the board's own notes above give its wider, pre-routed paths); vias 0.60 / 0.30 mm (JLC's standard via, no surcharge); copper >= 0.30 mm from the edge. JLC's 4-layer minimums are 0.09 / 0.09 mm and a 0.15 mm hole: nothing here is near them.
* **J1, the slot header, is through-hole and mounted on the BOTTOM side** (body against the bottom, pins down; soldered on the top side). Either JLC fits it (JLC stocks the part, LCSC C25503140; check on the order form that JLC accepts a through-hole part with its body on the bottom), or order J1 as do-not-place and hand-solder it: 40 joints from the top, the body flat against the bottom, square with the edge. The two halves only mate if the header is on the bottom (boards/coupon_common.py check_mating()). The 2541WV drawing limits soldering to 220 C for 10 s (PBT body), below a lead-free wave's temperature, and a wave or selective nozzle from the bottom would meet the body: the joints are soldered on the top side.
* **The 2 x 20 header has no key.** Plug a coupon with its SLOT EDGE / pin 1 silk over the motherboard socket's pin 1 and its post hole over the slot's spacer; turned 180 degrees, +5V meets GND pins (the slot switch limits the current). Never hot-plug: the slot's LED must be off (SPEC §3.5).
* **Not on the PCBA:** the M3 x 11 mm nylon spacer and screw on H1 (SPEC §9), bought separately.
* **Assembly size:** 58 x 60 mm (standard coupon) or 80 x 60 mm (wide) is under the 70 x 70 mm minimum this repository's caliper notes give for JLC's Standard PCBA (not re-checked for this board): if the order form asks for it, order with JLC's edge rails, or panelise, or use Economic PCBA if it takes the parts.
