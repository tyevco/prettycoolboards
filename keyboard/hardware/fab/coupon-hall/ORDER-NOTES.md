* **J1, the slot header, is keyed at pin 39 (the owner's decision D2 of 2026-11-27, SPEC §4.2) and fitted by hand: it is DNP for the assembler and in neither the JLC BOM nor the CPL.** The footprint has no pad and no hole at 39 (marked X on both silk layers), so a 2541WV-2x20P with all 40 pins does not go in, and jlcplace finds EasyEDA's pad 39 off copper (red). JLC listed no 2 x 20 header sold without pin 39 on 2026-11-27 (SPEC §4.2), so JLC cannot fit J1 as it comes (SPEC §11 #31). Buy it separately (LCSC C25503140, in the vendor-neutral BOM), pull pin 39 out of the header with pliers from the mating side, and solder it as below. Pin 39 is a corner pin: the inner row (away from the slot edge), at the end opposite pin 1, under the X. The header itself is symmetric, so pull any one corner pin and fit the header with the gap over the X; it goes in no other way round. This is the key's consequence; whether coupons then go as Economic PCBA is still the owner's open item (SPEC §11 #26, risk register recommendation 11, not decided). If a header sold without pin 39 is found, or JLC agrees to pull the pin, sheets/coupon_common.slot_sheet(header_dnp=False) puts J1 back in the JLC BOM and CPL.
* **J1 is through-hole and mounted on the BOTTOM side** (body against the bottom, pins down; soldered on the top side): 39 joints from the top, the body flat against the bottom, square with the edge. The two halves only mate if the header is on the bottom (boards/coupon_common.py check_mating()). The 2541WV drawing limits soldering to 220 C for 10 s (PBT body), below a lead-free wave's temperature, and a wave or selective nozzle from the bottom would meet the body: the joints are soldered on the top side. A 2 x 20 socket (the motherboard's, hole 39 plugged, with the slot's M3 spacer fitted, or a loose C5124634) holds the header square while its joints are soldered (risks.md SF-2).
* **Plugging a coupon in:** the SLOT EDGE / pin 1 silk over the motherboard socket's pin 1 and the post hole over the slot's spacer. Turned 180 degrees, the coupon's pin 2 meets the socket's plugged contact 39 and the coupon does not go in (the motherboard plugs hole 39; SPEC §4.2). Never hot-plug: the slot's LED must be off (SPEC §3.5).
