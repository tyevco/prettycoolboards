PrettyCool Keyboard concept

A planned analog keyboard designed as code: keys that also point, a thumb wheel whose feel follows the task, two wired hosts at once, and releases gated on wiring, DRC, latency and battery life. These are sketches of the owner's 2026-11-25 roadmap. The geometry is illustrative, the parts are candidates from desk research, and nothing is designed, built or measured.
