1. Outside-jaw deflection under the measuring force (beam theory)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Jaw: 7.8 thick (z), 20.0 wide (u) to y -8, tapering to 3.5 at the tip y -40; root at the rail face y -2.8.  F = 5.0 N in u.
Printed bodies lie back face down, so the jaw bends in the layer plane (in-layer stiffness).
material  contact y  bending um  shear um  total um  um per N
PETG            -37        17.0       4.3      21.4      4.27
PETG            -20         1.2       1.4       2.7      0.54
PETG             -6         0.0       0.2       0.2      0.05
PA-CF           -37         6.9       1.7       8.6      1.72
PA-CF           -20         0.5       0.6       1.1      0.22
PA-CF            -6         0.0       0.1       0.1      0.02
6061-T6         -37         0.4       0.1       0.4      0.09
6061-T6         -20         0.0       0.0       0.1      0.01
6061-T6          -6         0.0       0.0       0.0      0.00
  PETG: PETG E ~1.6-2.0 GPa, CTE 60-70 ppm/K (Bambu PETG HF / generic TDS)
  PA-CF: Bambu PAHT-CF E(XY) 4.2 GPa; CTE XY ~25-40 ppm/K (fibre-aligned, TVP)
  6061-T6: ASM: E 68.9 GPa, G 26 GPa, CTE 23.6 ppm/K
FEA cross-check (fea_jaw.py, print-stress solver, linear tets; jaw 7.8 thick, root 20.0; load spread over y -38..-32, so compare with y -37 / -35):
  PETG     at the load   15.7 um, tip   20.2 um
  PA-CF    at the load    6.3 um, tip    8.2 um
  6061-T6  at the load    0.4 um, tip    0.5 um
  (the FEA uses the datasheet E without the 0.85 FDM knock-down: scale it by 1/0.85 to compare)
What matters for accuracy is the force *variation*: a thumb gives 2-8 N, so the error band is
  PETG       4.3 um/N x 6 N spread =  25.6 um at the tip (NOT inside a 10 um share of the 30 um budget)
  PA-CF      1.7 um/N x 6 N spread =  10.3 um at the tip (NOT inside a 10 um share of the 30 um budget)
  6061-T6    0.1 um/N x 6 N spread =   0.5 um at the tip (inside a 10 um share of the 30 um budget)
Fixed jaw (printed PA-CF in rev A) has the same outline: add the same again for the closed loop.
Mitigation in rev A: PA-CF body; measure near the jaw root; a constant-force (slip-clutch) roller is a rev B item.

2. Slider yaw under the measuring force -> Y-gib preload
---------------------------------------------------------
The workpiece pushes the moving jaw with F = 5.0 N (+x) at y -37.  The equal and opposite drive
force acts ABOVE the reference edge, so the two form a couple (the drive arm is not zero):
drive applied at                                 drive y  M N.mm  P min N
thumb roller (friction drive at the beam edge)      20.0     285     3.50
thumb on the roller hub / axle                      23.9     304     3.73
thumb on the right end face, mid-height             35.0     360     4.42
(plunger levers about the strip's far end, u 78.0: sum 81.5 mm; the strip lifts at its LEFT end.)
Design: 6 N each -> margin 1.72x for the roller drive, 1.36x for an end-face push.
  (Review fix: the first version sized this on the jaw force alone, 2.27 N minimum, and set 4 N;
   with the drive couple 4 N is only 1.14x and below the end-face case.  Spring rate TVP.)
While seated, the yaw stiffness is the strip on the rail (brass on 1.8 mm printed rail): elastic-foundation estimate < 0.1 um at the jaw tip -- negligible next to section 1.

3. Slider roll under a press on the housing -> back-gib preload and sliding force
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ZERO/HOLD/MODE press (tact 1.6 N + finger): 2.5 N at y 31 -> 30 N.mm about the upper pad line (y 18.8); the back plungers at y 10.0 hold the lower pads down if P >= 1.73 N each
grip on the battery bay: 5.0 N at y 40 -> 106 N.mm about the upper pad line (y 18.8); the back plungers at y 10.0 hold the lower pads down if P >= 6.02 N each
Design: back plungers 4.0 N each.  A press beyond 3.3 N at the top edge (y 40) lifts the lower pads -- the reading is then wrong only while pressed (the gaps open, they do not close).
Sliding force: normal loads 8 N (back) + 12 N (Y) on brass/steel, mu ~0.2 (lightly oiled 0.12-0.2) -> 2 x 20 x 0.2 = 8.0 N to push the slider.  Typical caliper: 2-5 N.
(each preload acts on two faces, the plunger side and the reaction side, hence the factor 2.)

Back-cover check (review addition).  The back plungers sit in the back cover; their reaction
(8 N total) pushes the cover AWAY from the body.  Over the beam channel (y -1 .. 21.2) there is
no body material behind the beam, and the cover's only screws are at y 44, so the cover carries the
load as a cantilever from the screw line to the plungers:
  PETG   t 2.0, L 34, b 77.5: deflection  1.40 mm   whole width carries both plungers (lower bound)
  PETG   t 2.0, L 34, b 20.0: deflection  2.72 mm   a 20 mm strip per plunger (closer to real)
  PA-CF  t 2.0, L 34, b 77.5: deflection  0.57 mm   whole width carries both plungers (lower bound)
  PA-CF  t 2.0, L 34, b 20.0: deflection  1.10 mm   a 20 mm strip per plunger (closer to real)
  -> the plungers' ~1 mm working travel goes into bending the cover: the back-gib preload (and so the
     pad seating of section 3) is NOT held as designed.  Needs a cover fastener near y 0..20 (doc section 4).

4. Thermal error (zeroed at T0, read at T0 + dT)
-------------------------------------------------
Chain from the moving-jaw face to the AS5311: 11 mm of body (jaw face -> board boss at u 11) + 28 mm of FR4 (boss -> AS5311 at u 39).
Fixed jaw: 5 mm of body from the dowels (x -5) to the face.  The tape is bonded to the beam (follows the steel).
body     beam     slider um/K fixed um/K  scale um/K @150 total 10 K um
PETG     O1             0.767      0.095           -0.075           9.4
PETG     1.4301         0.572      0.070            0.675          13.2
PA-CF    O1             0.349      0.095           -0.075           5.2
PA-CF    1.4301         0.154      0.070            0.675           9.0
6061-T6  O1             0.279      0.063           -0.075           4.2
6061-T6  1.4301         0.084      0.038            0.675           8.0
Hand heat warms the slider 5-10 K within minutes (TVP); re-zero after handling.  The scale term is the classic
one: a 1.4301 beam reads a steel part short by 0.7 um/K at 150 mm -- O1 matches steel workpieces.
A printed body also creeps: PETG under the gib springs relaxes over days, which changes preload, not position
(the gaps are set by brass pads on set screws), but a creeping boss moves the board: use PA-CF for the body.

5. Gap stack-up (z, mm)
------------------------
Nominal chain (beam front face = 0):
  board bottom (electrode face)        +0.150   set by the 4 front-pad set screws
  scale face                           +0.000   pocket 0.85 = PCB 0.8 + adhesive 0.05
  AS5311 face                          -0.950   A nominal 1.1
  tape face                            -1.250   channel 2.00 deep - shim 0.10 (nominal A) - adhesive 0.05 - tape 0.6
  -> capacitive gap 0.150, magnetic gap 0.300

(a) Capacitive gap, NOT adjusted per unit (pads set to a fixed screw depth):
    scale PCB thickness (FR4 0.8 +/-10 %)              +/-0.080  JLC standard; 'tight' option +/-0.05
    scale adhesive 467MP                               +/-0.010  3M datasheet 0.05 nominal
    scale pocket depth                                 +/-0.020  machined
    board bow over the 40 x 5 mm electrode area        +/-0.030  0.075 % of 40 mm, a tenth of the IPC limit (TVP)
    printed boss height / pad pocket depth             +/-0.100  FDM +/-0.1
    beam face flatness along travel                    +/-0.010  ground stock, drawing PCC-M-02
    worst case                                         +/-0.250
    RSS                                                +/-0.134
  -> the 0.15 gap cannot be built open-loop: worst case is larger than the gap itself.
  (board thickness is not in this chain: the board bottom seats on the bosses.)

(b) Capacitive gap AFTER the per-unit setting (dial indicator on the board top, 2 um, beam face as datum):
  board-height terms (shared with the AS5311 gap):
    indicator + board-thickness micrometer             +/-0.005  
    board bow over the electrode area (unmeasured)     +/-0.030  TVP: 0.075 %/40 mm; IPC-6012 limit = 0.30
    beam face flatness along travel                    +/-0.010  
    set-screw setting resolution (M2 x 0.4, 10 deg)    +/-0.011  0.4 x 10/360
    thermal: 5.4 mm PETG tower, +/-10 K                +/-0.004  
    pad / body creep and wear (PETG, first months)     +/-0.020  TVP; PA-CF ~half
    worst case                                         +/-0.080
    RSS                                                +/-0.039
  plus the scale face:
    indicator + board-thickness micrometer             +/-0.005  
    board bow over the electrode area (unmeasured)     +/-0.030  TVP: 0.075 %/40 mm; IPC-6012 limit = 0.30
    beam face flatness along travel                    +/-0.010  
    set-screw setting resolution (M2 x 0.4, 10 deg)    +/-0.011  0.4 x 10/360
    thermal: 5.4 mm PETG tower, +/-10 K                +/-0.004  
    pad / body creep and wear (PETG, first months)     +/-0.020  TVP; PA-CF ~half
    scale thickness / flushness along its length       +/-0.020  one PCB: thickness varies less than lot-to-lot (TVP)
    worst case                                         +/-0.100
    RSS                                                +/-0.044
  -> 0.15 +/- 0.044 RSS (0.100 worst): the electrodes never touch at RSS, but the
     signal swings ~+/-30 % with gap along the travel -- fine for the experimental phase-ratio readout.
  CAUTION: the board is held only by bosses at y >= 24.5; its lower band (electrodes y 10.6-17.5, AS5311
  at y 6) overhangs 7-24 mm with no support.  The +/-0.03 bow term is a tenth of the IPC-6012 class 2
  limit (0.75 %, i.e. 0.30 over the 40 mm electrode length, twice the gap): flatness-check every slider
  board over its lower band and reject boards worse than 0.03 mm, or the electrodes can touch the scale.

6. AS5311 gap budget (SPEC 4.1: 0.30 +/- 0.10)
-----------------------------------------------
(a) Open-loop (no measurement of the package or the tape):
    AS5311 seated height A (0.85..1.20, JEDEC MO-153)  +/-0.175  centre 1.025 vs model 1.10
    solder standoff                                    +/-0.030  
    tape thickness                                     +/-0.050  TVP; Dexter quotes +/-0.15 on its strips
    tape adhesive                                      +/-0.010  
    channel depth                                      +/-0.020  machined, from face D
    board height as set (5b board terms, RSS)          +/-0.039  the board height is set for the cap gap
    worst case                                         +/-0.324
    RSS                                                +/-0.190
  -> +/-0.190 RSS is OUTSIDE the +/-0.10 window.
(b) With the per-unit measurement in assembly (mechanical 6): micrometer the board + AS5311 stack before
    fitting (gives A to +/-0.005), depth-gauge the tape face below the beam face after bonding (+/-0.005),
    and lay 0-0.35 mm of Kapton shim (0.025 steps) under the tape so the predicted gap is 0.30.
    The channel (2.00 deep) gives 0.30 with NO shim at A max 1.20; a shim raises the tape,
    so every shorter package can be corrected (A nominal 1.10 -> 0.10 shim, A min 0.85 -> 0.35).
    [Review fix: the first channel (1.90) gave 0.20 unshimmed at A max, which a shim can only make smaller.]
    A measured                                         +/-0.005  
    tape face measured                                 +/-0.005  
    shim step / 2                                      +/-0.013  0.025 steps
    tape thickness along the length                    +/-0.020  TVP
    channel depth along the length                     +/-0.010  
    board height as set (5b board terms, RSS)          +/-0.039  
    worst case                                         +/-0.092
    RSS                                                +/-0.048
    true worst case (board terms worst case too)       +/-0.132
  -> 0.30 +/- 0.048 RSS: inside 0.2-0.4 (2.1x the RSS).
     The true worst case, +/-0.132, is OUTSIDE the window, so tolerancing does not
     guarantee the gap; the firmware field check below accepts or rejects each unit.
     [Review fix: the first version put the 5b RSS into this worst-case sum and reported 0.003 margin.]
Field check in firmware: AS5311 MagINCn / MagDECn both high = field in range; the SSI status bits
(OCF, COF, LIN) flag an out-of-range gap.  Bring-up records them at R = 0, 75, 150.

7. Envelope
------------
Housing envelope (u, y, z): 77.5 x 52.0 x 16.0 mm (SPEC 7: <= 78 x 52 x 16); thumb roller rim at u 78.6 (0.6 proud of the end face).
SPEC 7 counts the roller in the 78 mm: the rim reaches u 78.6, 0.6 mm over (measured from the jaw face u 0).
Tape channel 2.00 deep leaves 2.00 mm of steel under it (cross-section area drops to 80 %; bending is not loaded in use).
